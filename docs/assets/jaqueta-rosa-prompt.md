# Jaqueta rosa — recorte para lookbook

Criado com o imagegen integrado, modo edit/background-extraction, transparent_background=true. Não foi usado CLI/API fallback.

Entrada: public/images/pitali-hero-campanha.jpg (foto original da cliente).

Saída de trabalho: assets/originals/jaqueta-rosa-recorte.png.
Saída preparada para o site: public/images/jaqueta-rosa-recorte.webp, 1312x1199, alpha real, codificação lossless.

Partes cobertas pela modelo e pelo cabelo foram reconstruídas pela IA. A silhueta, as costuras, o zíper e os detalhes ocultos precisam ser conferidos pela cliente antes de uso comercial. O ativo não está aprovado para venda nem publicado em um catálogo.

## Prompt exato

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: supplied photograph of two PITALI models at the beach volleyball net. Extract ONLY the bright rose/fuchsia pink cropped long-sleeve zip jacket worn by the model on the LEFT. Create one premium ecommerce cutout of this actual garment, isolated on genuine transparent background. No model, body, face, hair, hands, headband, shorts, net, scenery, hanger or mannequin visible. Preserve the garment's actual fuchsia pink color, material, zipper, panel construction, elastic gathered cuffs and hem, and cropped silhouette. Show front-facing ghost mannequin garment volume with both sleeves visible, centered, generous transparent margins, no cast shadow. Remove the wearer's body and hair; where hair/body occludes fabric, reconstruct only the minimal matching fabric necessary, with conservative plain stitching; do not invent pockets, branding, labels, text, logos or redesign details. Keep it a faithful photo-derived garment, not a fashion illustration. Do not extract the gray jacket on the right. High-resolution crisp but natural fabric texture, clean antialiased alpha edges without white/gray halo. Output a single jacket only.
